Abstract

While diffusion models excel at image generation, their denoising trajectories pass through corrupted intermediate states far from the image manifold. We ask whether generation can instead proceed through recognizable images throughout the entire trajectory.

We introduce a generative framework built around a variation-reducing degradation process that contracts diverse images toward a shared prototype while preserving recognizable intermediate states. We distill the reverse process into a diversification operator that transforms the prototype into increasingly varied samples. At inference, every step yields a recognizable image, allowing early termination and enabling a controllable speed–variation trade-off.

Manifold Traversal

Intermediate states remain valid and clean on the data manifold throughout generation, unlike noisy diffusion trajectories.

Speed–Variation Trade-Off

Fewer steps yield samples close to the prototype quickly; more steps expand output diversity on demand.

Noise & Variation Perturbation

Conditioned on associated noise latents η with perturbation mechanisms to prevent prediction error accumulation.

Method Overview

Our framework operates in two stages: (1) Variation-Reducing Degradation via DiffMorpher image morphing to construct trajectories connecting diverse teacher-generated samples to a representative CLIP prototype, and (2) Reverse Distillation to train a diversification operator with initial noise conditioning \(\eta\) and noise perturbation \(\tau\). 

Algorithm 1: Training Speed-Variation Distillation
Require: Trajectory dataset D, model ε_θ, noise perturbation τ, variation perturbation s_max
1: while not converged do
2:    Sample (z_t, z_{t-1}, η, t) ~ D
3:    Sample perturbation s ~ {0, ..., s_max - 1}
4:    Set z_dot_t ← z_{t+s}
5:    Perturb z_dot_t to obtain z_dot_t^τ using Forward Diffusion (Eq. 1)
6:    Set t_tilde ← t + τ
7:    Predict noise residual ε_hat_t ← ε_θ(concat(z_dot_t^τ, η), t_tilde)
8:    Recover z_hat_{t-1} via clean prediction formula
9:    Compute Loss L = E[ || z_hat_{t-1} - z_{t-1} ||_1 ]
10:   Update θ
11: end while
Algorithm 2: Inference & Controllable Diversification
Require: Trained model ε_θ, noise perturbation τ, target step T
1: Sample noise conditioning η ~ N(0, I)
2: Initialize prototype latent z_T from ε_θ
3: for t = T, T-1, ..., 1 do
4:    Perturb z_t to obtain z_t^τ
5:    Set t_tilde ← t + τ
6:    Predict noise ε_hat_t ← ε_θ(concat(z_t^τ, η), t_tilde)
7:    Recover z_{t-1}
8: end for
9: Decode {z_t} via VAE decoder to obtain clean image trajectory {x_t}
10: return {x_t} (Each step yields a valid, clean sample!)
